# NestHome Privacy Notice

**Effective Date:** September 21, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 21, 2026

## 1. Our Commitment to Your Family’s Privacy

NestHome is designed to help families remain connected to their child’s experience at The Little Nest Playschool.

Through NestHome, parents and guardians may be able to see information such as their child’s attendance, activities, learning experiences, teacher-recorded moments, photos, videos, artwork, progress, memories, communications, and other information connected with their child’s time at The Little Nest.

Because much of this information relates to children, we recognize that it deserves particular care.

This Privacy Notice explains what personal data we collect and process, why we use it, how it may be shared, how we protect it, how long we keep it, and the rights available to parents, guardians, children, and other users.

NestHome is operated by **Baruel-Stoyko Educational Consulting, doing business under the name The Little Nest Playschool**, referred to in this Notice as **“The Little Nest,” “we,” “our,” or “us.”**

For the personal data processed through NestHome and our connected systems, The Little Nest acts as the Personal Information Controller where it determines the purposes and means of processing.

We process personal data in accordance with applicable Philippine privacy laws, including Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the **Data Privacy Act of 2012**, its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and applicable issuances of the National Privacy Commission.

---

## 2. Who This Privacy Notice Applies To

This Privacy Notice applies to personal data processed through NestHome and related Little Nest systems concerning:

- parents and legal guardians;
- children enrolled or participating at The Little Nest;
- authorized family members or other individuals given NestHome access;
- individuals communicating with The Little Nest through NestHome;
- individuals appearing in photos, videos, artwork, messages, or other materials stored within the system; and
- users of NestHome whose technical or usage information is collected when they use the service.

NestHome is primarily intended for parents, guardians, and other adults who have been appropriately authorized to access information about a child.

If The Little Nest later introduces features intended to be used directly by children, appropriate child-friendly privacy information and parental involvement will be provided where required.

Depending on the type of information and the purpose involved, The Little Nest may rely on one or more lawful criteria recognized under applicable Philippine privacy law.

These may include:

- your consent;
- processing necessary in connection with a service or contractual relationship;
- compliance with a legal obligation;
- protection of vital interests such as life, health, or safety;

- protection or establishment of lawful rights and interests.

Sensitive personal information, including certain information concerning a child’s education or health, is processed only where a lawful basis permitted under applicable law exists.

Where processing requires consent, we will seek appropriate consent for the specific purpose involved.

Where consent is optional, refusing or withdrawing that consent will not prevent unrelated services from being provided unless the information is genuinely necessary for those services.

## 7. Children’s Personal Data

Children are at the center of NestHome.

We recognize that children have privacy rights of their own even when their parent or guardian is responsible for making decisions on their behalf.

We therefore seek to collect and use children’s information only where it has a genuine purpose connected with their Little Nest experience.

We aim to apply additional care when handling children’s information, including through:

- limited and purpose-based access;
- parental or guardian involvement where appropriate;
- age-appropriate practices;
- restricted access to child-related information;
- careful handling of photographs and videos;
- appropriate technical and organizational safeguards; and
- review of new features that could create additional privacy risks for children.

NestHome is not intended to create a public profile for a child.

A child’s NestHome information is intended to remain within the family and Little Nest environment except where disclosure is authorized, required to provide the service, separately consented to, or otherwise permitted by law.

## 8. Photos, Videos and Other Media

Photographs and videos are an important part of NestHome because they allow parents to see and preserve real moments from their child’s Little Nest experience.

Teachers and other authorized personnel may capture and upload media during:

- regular sessions;
- activities;
- projects;
- celebrations;
- special events; and
- other appropriate Little Nest experiences.

Media may be associated with the child or children appearing in it and made available to their authorized families.

### Group Photos and Videos

Some classroom or activity media may contain more than one child.

Where group media is made available through NestHome, it may be visible to the authorized families of children legitimately associated with that photograph, video, event, activity, or moment.

We seek to limit access to people with an appropriate connection to the media.

Parents and authorized family members are expected to respect the privacy of all children appearing in NestHome content and should not misuse, publicly redistribute, or commercially use images of another child without appropriate authority.

### NestHome Media Is Not Automatic Marketing Consent

The use of a child’s photograph, video, artwork, or other content inside NestHome for educational, family, operational, or memory-related purposes does **not by itself constitute consent for unrelated advertising or public marketing use**.

Where The Little Nest wishes to use identifiable child media for advertising, public social media, promotional materials, or other marketing purposes requiring separate authorization, appropriate permission will be obtained where required.

## 16. How Long We Keep Personal Data

We do not necessarily use the same retention period for every type of information.

Personal data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for another lawful and compatible purpose, or as required or permitted by applicable law.

Factors we may consider when determining retention include:

- whether the child remains enrolled;
- whether the NestHome or family account remains active;
- the purpose for which the information was collected;
- whether the information forms part of an educational, enrollment, accounting, payment, or administrative record;
- whether the information forms part of the family’s NestHome memories;
- legal or regulatory requirements;
- potential disputes or legal claims;
- safety and security requirements; and
- requests made by the parent, guardian, child, or other data subject.

Some operational or administrative records may need to be retained after a child leaves The Little Nest because of legal, accounting, contractual, or legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

Family memory content may have a different retention period because preserving the child’s experiences and memories is one of the purposes of NestHome.

Where information is no longer necessary and there is no lawful reason to retain it, we will securely delete, destroy, anonymize, or otherwise dispose of it using an appropriate method.

Certain information may temporarily remain in backups or system archives until those backups are securely overwritten or deleted in the ordinary course of system operations.

## 17. Your Privacy Rights

Individuals whose personal data is processed have rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, subject to applicable conditions and limitations.

Depending on the circumstances, these may include the right to:

### Be Informed

You have the right to know whether personal data about you or your child is being collected or processed and to receive appropriate information about that processing.

### Access Personal Data

You may request access to personal data that we process about you or, where you are legally authorized, your child.

### Correct Inaccurate Information

You may ask us to correct personal data that is inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or misleading.

### Object to Processing

You may object to certain types of processing where that right applies.

### Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent.

Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawfully performed before the consent was withdrawn and may not apply where another lawful basis permits or requires continued processing.

### Request Erasure or Blocking

You may request the deletion, removal, blocking, or destruction of personal data where the conditions under applicable law are met.

This right is not absolute. We may need to retain certain information where continued retention is required or permitted by law or is necessary for legitimate legal or administrative purposes.

### Data Portability

Where applicable, you may have the right to obtain personal data in a structured and commonly used format or otherwise exercise applicable data portability rights.

### File a Complaint

You have the right to raise a privacy concern with The Little Nest and may also file a complaint with the **National Privacy Commission of the Philippines** where appropriate.

### Claim Damages

Where applicable under law, a data subject may have rights relating to damages resulting from inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of personal data.

## 26. Our Privacy Principle

NestHome exists to bring families closer to the meaningful experiences their children have at The Little Nest.

The information entrusted to NestHome should therefore be used with the same purpose in mind: to support the child, connect the family, operate The Little Nest responsibly, and preserve meaningful experiences while respecting the privacy and dignity of every child and family.
